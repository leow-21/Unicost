University cost using all factors

Why is this tool better than a simple calculator?

At first, just using the tuition fee and rent fee may seem to be an adequate estimate - but costs pile up as a student. This tool is designed to give the most transparent estimate taking into account factors most would gloss over, and help students be most prepared for their potential spending. As a student myself I wanted to create a tool to help others in my position who may not have been aware of the full costs before living independently, and hope this can do just that.
